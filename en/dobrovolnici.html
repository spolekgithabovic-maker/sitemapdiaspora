Volunteering in Brno

Help foreigners in Brno as a volunteer

Diaspora Care z.s. is a non-profit association from Brno helping foreigners in Czechia with adaptation and integration. You don't need to be an expert — we value your time, languages and willingness to help.

How it works

Three simple steps

1. Fill in the form

Tell us which languages you speak, how you'd like to help and when it suits you.

2. We'll get in touch

We'll email you and agree on what works for you.

3. Help when you can

Weekdays, evenings or weekends — whatever fits your schedule.

Transparency & trust

We are open about who we are and how our work is organised.

Official status

Diaspora Care z. s. is an association (spolek) entered in the register of associations kept by the Regional Court in Brno (L 32394), IČO 30061563. We are non-profit and non-partisan.

Where the money goes

We are funded by subsidies, grants and donations. Profit is never shared among members or management — all funds go solely to our statutory purpose.

Cooperation

We are open to cooperation with public authorities, healthcare facilities, schools, employers and other non-profit organisations.
