Who we are

About us

Diaspora Care z.s. is a non-profit, non-partisan association (spolek) based in Brno. We help foreigners in Czechia adapt and become part of society, protect their rights and dignity, and support good neighbourly relations with local people.

Some of us started our own lives in Czechia from scratch. That's why we know how much it matters to have someone by your side who will listen and point the way.

Support

We listen, give advice, and stay by your side in difficult situations.

Integration

We help you understand institutions, the language, and everyday life in Czechia.

Opportunities

We support you in finding housing, work, and your place in a new society.

Areas

How we help

Choose a topic to learn more. If you're not sure where to start, just write to us.

Finding your way in Czechia

Institutions, accompaniment, legal questions

Institutions and systems

We help you understand how public offices, healthcare, social security and education work in Czechia, where to go, and what to prepare.

Interpreting and accompaniment

Interpreting and translation in everyday situations, and accompaniment to offices, doctors and other institutions — provided by qualified professionals.

Basic legal orientation

We help you get your bearings on a legal question and find a professional lawyer if you need one.

Housing and work

Finding housing, keeping your home, the job market

Housing

Advice and help with finding suitable housing, dealing with landlords, and preventing the loss of your home, including material support.

Job market orientation

We help you find your way around the Czech job market: where to look for work and how to start.

Support in hard times

Psychosocial help, accompaniment during treatment

Psychosocial support and art therapy

Psychological consultations, art therapy and support programmes for people who have been through trauma or another serious life experience — provided by qualified professionals.

By your side during treatment

Support for people undergoing cancer or other demanding treatment, and for their loved ones: accompaniment and help during treatment, surgery and rehabilitation.

Families, children and school

Education and life in a new environment

School and education

We help migrant families, children and young people enter the education system, communicate with schools, and understand Czech culture and surroundings.

Language and community

Czech practice, dialogue between cultures and generations

Czech language practice

We organise meetups to practise Czech — in a calm and friendly atmosphere.

Dialogue and good neighbours

We support dialogue between generations and cultures, and good neighbourly relations between foreigners and the local community.

Official status

Diaspora Care z. s. is an association (spolek) entered in the register of associations kept by the Regional Court in Brno (L 32394), IČO 30061563. We are non-profit and non-partisan.

Where the money goes

We are funded by subsidies, grants and donations. Profit is never shared among members or management — all funds go solely to our statutory purpose.

Cooperation

We are open to cooperation with public authorities, healthcare facilities, schools, employers and other non-profit organisations.
